What "broker" means here

LA Jet Charter is an air charter broker/agent under 14 CFR Part 295. We arrange flights on your behalf; we do not own, lease, or operate any aircraft, and we are not an air carrier. Every flight booked through us is performed by an independent, FAA-certificated Part 135 air carrier that holds full operational control of the aircraft, crew, and flight — including the final go/no-go decision — at all times.

Pricing

Every price shown on this site is a "starting at" estimate. Final pricing is confirmed once an operator quote is accepted, before any payment is collected. We never present a locked or guaranteed fare ahead of that confirmation.

Hotel & package inclusions

Where a Signature Route Package includes a hotel stay, that value — along with any small activation/resort fee charged by the hotel program — is included in your quoted price. We never advertise a hotel stay as "free" separately from the price you're quoted.

Insurance

LA Jet Charter maintains errors & omissions insurance appropriate to an air charter broker. Policy details are available on request.
