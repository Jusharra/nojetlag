Information we collect

When you request a quote, we collect your name, email, phone number, and trip details (route, dates, passenger count, aircraft preference, notes). We store this information in our booking system (Airtable) to prepare and follow up on your quote.

How we use it

We use your information to source charter quotes on your behalf, communicate with you about your trip, process payment once you accept a quote, and — only if you opt in — send you SMS or email updates about future empty-leg deals or offers.

Who we share it with

We share only the trip details necessary to source a quote with prospective charter operators. We do not sell your personal information.

Payment information

Payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details on our own systems.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS/email marketing at any time. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact us using the details on this site.
