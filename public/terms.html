1. Who we are

LA Jet Charter ("we", "us") is an air charter broker/agent. We arrange private aviation charter flights on behalf of customers with independent, FAA-certificated Part 135 air carriers. We are not an air carrier and do not operate aircraft.

2. Booking & pricing

All prices displayed on this site are "starting at" estimates. A final price is confirmed in writing once an operator quote is accepted and before payment is collected. Quotes are subject to aircraft and operator availability at time of confirmation.

3. Payment

Payment is collected by LA Jet Charter on behalf of the arrangement described above. Funds are held and disbursed to the operating carrier in accordance with California Seller of Travel trust account requirements.

4. Cancellations & changes

Cancellation and change terms are set by the operating carrier for each booking and will be disclosed to you at the time your quote is confirmed.

5. Limitation of liability

LA Jet Charter's role is limited to arranging your charter. Operational control of the aircraft, crew, and flight rests solely with the operating Part 135 carrier at all times.

6. Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to our team via the contact details on this site.
